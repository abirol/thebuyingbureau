This may include information such as your browser type, device type, approximate location, referring website, pages visited, time spent on the site, IP address and similar technical data.

We do not ask for sensitive personal information through this website unless it is genuinely necessary for a feature or service.

Information collected through the site may be used to operate and maintain the website, understand how visitors use it, improve our articles and site experience, respond to messages, prevent fraud or abuse, measure advertising or marketing performance, and comply with legal obligations.

Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to maintain records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, or meet legal requirements.

Your choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including the right to request access, correction or deletion of certain information.

You may also be able to limit cookies through your browser settings or through any cookie controls provided on this site.

Affiliate disclosure

Some links on The Buying Bureau Co. are affiliate links.

That means if you click one of those links and make a purchase, we may receive a commission from the company or retailer. This does not increase the price you pay.

Affiliate commissions help support the cost of running and publishing this site.

An affiliate relationship does not guarantee positive coverage. We aim to explain both the useful parts of a product or service and the reasons it may not be right for everyone.

Not every link on the site is an affiliate link, and not every product or service we write about pays us a commission.

When an article contains affiliate links, we also include a disclosure within that article so you do not have to hunt through this page to find out.

If we ever publish content that has been directly sponsored or paid for by a company, it will be identified clearly.

Questions about our affiliate relationships can be sent to hello@thebuyingbureau.co.
